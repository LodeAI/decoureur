Reporting a mistake in the data

That is the most useful thing you can send. Include the rider's name, what is wrong and where it is right. Such reports go into the next data round.

The subscription

Cancelling, refunds and payment history all run through Apple and not through us — we cannot reach them. On your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. If that does not work, Apple Support can help you further.

Today's puzzle will not load

That usually means the puzzle schedule in your version of the app has run out. Update the app in the App Store; the new version brings a new schedule. Your results and your streak stay.

Press

Writing about cycling or about apps and want material? Email us; there is a short press kit with screenshots and a description.
